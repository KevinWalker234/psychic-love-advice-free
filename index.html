How We Read

Five Ways We Read Your Heart

Tarot

Tarot reveals the emotional currents running beneath your relationship — the tensions, hopes, and turning points words alone can't always capture.

Why choose tarot?

  • Visual, story-driven insight into your situation
  • Cards that reflect both challenges and opportunities
  • A structured way to explore 'what if' questions
  • Useful for decisions that feel emotionally tangled

A tarot reading works well when you need a fresh angle on a familiar problem.

Book a Tarot Reading

Numerology

Numerology looks at the numbers already present in your life — birth date, name, key dates — to highlight compatibility patterns and timing.

Why choose numerology?

  • A logical, pattern-based lens on compatibility
  • Insight into timing for major relationship decisions
  • A grounded complement to more intuitive methods
  • Clear, repeatable calculations behind every insight

Numerology suits readers who like their guidance backed by structure.

Book a Numerology Reading

Astrology

Astrology examines how your chart and your partner's interact — where you naturally align, and where extra patience helps.

Why choose astrology?

  • A detailed map of compatibility strengths and friction points
  • Context for recurring relationship dynamics
  • Timing insight tied to real astrological transits
  • A shared language for talking through your differences

Astrology gives language to dynamics you've likely already been sensing.

Book an Astrology Reading

Palmistry

Palmistry reads the lines already written in your hand — a hands-on, deeply personal way to talk through matters of the heart.

Why choose palmistry?

  • A tactile, in-the-moment reading experience
  • Insight drawn from a long-standing traditional practice
  • A grounding option if this is your first reading
  • Guidance that feels immediate and personal

Palmistry is a good starting point if reading guidance is new to you.

Book a Palmistry Reading

Dream Analysis

Recurring dreams about an ex, a wedding, or someone new are rarely random — dream analysis helps translate what your subconscious is already telling you.

Why choose dream analysis?

  • Insight into what recurring dreams may represent
  • A gentle way to process unresolved feelings
  • Useful when the same dream keeps returning unprompted
  • No need to interpret the symbolism on your own

Bring the details you remember — even fragments are enough to start with.
